### Q&A preparation (be ready to answer)
1. *Is Uzbekistan ready for full self-driving?* — Not for Level 5 yet (cost, law, maps), but
   ready to start with Level 2 and pilot shuttles.
2. *What about jobs?* — Some driving jobs shrink, but new IT, data and maintenance jobs appear.
3. *Why not just import the technology?* — Local mapping, laws and language/road conditions
   must be adapted; that is where local IT specialists add value.
